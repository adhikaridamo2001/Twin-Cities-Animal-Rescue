Help a pet find a safe, loving home

We connect animals in need with adopters, foster families, and neighbors who care. Every conversation, donation, and volunteer hour helps pets take their next step.

Our mission

Twin Cities Animal Rescue works with community members to support pet adoption, temporary foster care, and practical help for animals waiting for a home.

Our community impact

Our impact grows through the dedication of adopters, foster families, and volunteers.

How you can help

  • Adopt a pet who is ready for a permanent home.
  • Offer a temporary foster home while an animal waits for adoption.
  • Volunteer your time or share the rescue's message with a neighbor.
