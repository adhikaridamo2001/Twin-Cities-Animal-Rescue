Adoption, foster care, and volunteering

There are several ways to support an animal while it waits for a permanent home. Our team can help you learn which option is right for you.

Adoption process

  1. Review available pets and ask questions about an animal's needs.
  2. Complete an adoption inquiry so the team can learn about your household.
  3. Talk with a rescue coordinator about a good match and next steps.

Foster program

Foster families provide temporary care, help pets feel comfortable in a home, and share observations that can help with adoption matching. A coordinator can explain current needs and available support.

Ask about becoming a foster.

Volunteer roles

  • Help with animal care and enrichment.
  • Support adoption events and community outreach.
  • Assist with transport, supplies, or administrative tasks when needed.

Role availability can change. Share your interests and schedule on the volunteer and foster interest form.
